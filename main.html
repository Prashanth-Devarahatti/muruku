Our Story

From a beloved street food tradition to a growing brand

The Concept

The Murukuu Sandwich — Chennai's Gift

Originating in the bustling lanes of Chennai's Sowcarpet neighbourhood, the Murukuu Sandwich is a uniquely Indian street food innovation. Instead of bread, crispy spiral murukku (chakli) serves as the base and top, sandwiching fresh vegetables, chutneys, and cheese in between.

👨‍🍳
Co-Founder

PKPR Prem Pavan Kumar

With a passion for authentic street food and a vision to bring healthy snacking to the people of Nellore, Prem Pavan Kumar co-founded Mr. Murukuu Sandwich — bringing the flavours of Chennai to Andhra Pradesh.
